Singapore salary purchasing power

Singapore has a Numbeo cost-of-living index (excluding rent) of 87.7 on our scale where New York ≈ 100. Use the calculator with Singapore as your home city to see equivalent monthly pay and spending power in destinations worldwide.

Local context

Typical gross monthly pay in Singapore is about 5,800 S$ (approximate benchmark). The calculator also shows how you compare to that local average when you pick a destination — using approximate FX to put salaries on a comparable footing.

Good to know

  • Indices are directional, refreshed periodically from Numbeo.
  • Rent is excluded from the index; housing can swing real budgets.
  • Taxes, benefits, and visa rules are not modeled — see methodology.
